# Chronicles of Kilwa - Release Notes

All notable changes to Chronicles of Kilwa are documented here. Newest release first.
Chronicles of Kilwa is a VR journey through the story of Kilwa Kisiwani for Meta Quest 3.

## [0.2.2] - 2026-11-24
The Arrival: sail with the Captain from the open sea to Kilwa in the year 960, bring the chief a gift, and speak for the Captain before him.

### Added
- The Arrival, the first chapter. You wake on the Captain's ship beside the time lantern, help raise the sail, arrive at Kilwa and stand before its chief.
- The Captain and his crew speak to you at every step, and remind you if you lose your way.
- A yellow arrow and a card show where to go and what to do next.
- Kilwa first appears far off on the horizon. Raise the sail and watch the island come closer.
- Look through the spyglass to find the island.
- Pull the sail rope down and tie it on the cleat: the card shows how.
- Carry the chief's gift, a painted crate, from the ship straight to the mat before him.
- The chief of Kilwa wears a crown of palm leaves, with two guards standing beside him, spears in hand.
- Speak for the Captain: you put his words and the chief's into a language both understand.

### Changed
- The time lantern glows from its gems without lighting up the people around it.
- The lantern's ring of gems shows five moments in Kilwa's story, from the arrival in 960 to the island today.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-24
The first voyage: the Captain's ship in Kilwa's harbour.

### Added
- Stand on the deck of a dhow in Kilwa's harbour.
- The time lantern: turn its ring of gems to see moments from Kilwa's story.
- A spyglass to look out over the coast.
- Raise the sail and the ship casts off.
